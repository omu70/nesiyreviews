Production-ready pass: security fixes, real pagination, image pipeline, tests

Audit and completion pass over the existing Remix + Supabase + Polaris app.
The architecture is unchanged — managed installation, token exchange, sessions
in Postgres and the signed App Proxy were already right and were kept.

Security
- Drop the RLS policy that let any holder of the anon key read every approved
  review in the database, across every shop, and the storage policy that let
  them write into the review-image bucket
- Drop shops.store_key and the entire retired snippet distribution path
- Cap request bodies, rate-limit signed-URL minting, and keep database error
  text out of every storefront response

Correctness
- products/update matched a column holding a handle, not an id, so renaming a
  product silently detached its reviews; reviews now carry the numeric id
- The aggregate trigger left a stale rating on the old handle after a rename
- Store-wide reviews were mixed into every product page, so the product card
  and the product page disagreed on the count; now a setting, off by default
- The Liquid and JavaScript review-count formatters disagreed, producing a
  visible flicker on every page load

Features
- Client-side image resizing with separate thumbnails, an accessible
  swipeable lightbox, and a graceful fallback for photos that fail to load
- Display limit enforced in Postgres with range pagination, plus load-more or
  numbered pages
- Rating distribution, storefront filters, sort, and a complete submission
  form with title and email
- Product rating badge, product reviews and store reviews theme app blocks
- reviews.rating / reviews.rating_count metafield sync
- products/delete webhook, and delivery de-duplication on every handler

Verification
- 11 unit tests, 77 browser checks against the real widget in Chromium
  (desktop and mobile), 27 database checks applying every migration twice
- ESLint config added; lint and production build both clean
